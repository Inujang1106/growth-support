Support

IronLog is a personal workout and nutrition log with no sign-in. All your records are stored only on this iPhone. The app interface is currently in Korean.

Before changing devices or deleting the app, save a backup in Settings > Backup & Restore > Save JSON backup and keep the file somewhere like iCloud Drive. On a new device, restore it with Restore JSON backup.

For help, email hybexofficial@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue.
